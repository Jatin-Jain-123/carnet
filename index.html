What is Carnet?

Everyday document chores shouldn't mean uploading private files to a website. Carnet is a calm, paper-like desktop app that handles the common jobs, merging PDFs, shrinking files for email, turning a PDF into an editable Word document, converting a phone photo, all without your files ever leaving your computer.

Pick what you're working with (PDF, Word document or image), pick a tool, drop your file in, and download the result. No sign-up, no watermarks, no waiting in an online queue.

What it can do

PDF
  • Merge several PDFs; drag them into order first.
  • Reorder pages by dragging page thumbnails.
  • Compress by a percentage or to an exact target size.
  • Convert to Word with editable text and pictures.
Word document
  • Compress by optimising pictures; text untouched.
  • Convert to PDF; uses Microsoft Word for a perfect match when it's installed.
  • Reorder pages by dragging page previews.
Image
  • Compress by a percentage or to a target size (great for upload limits).
  • Convert between JPEG, PNG, WebP, AVIF, GIF, TIFF, BMP, ICO and PDF.
  • Opens iPhone HEIC photos and SVG drawings too.
  • Private by design: everything runs on your PC, even without internet.
  • Hits your size: tell it "under 2 MB" and it finds the best quality that fits.
  • Light and dark: follows your Windows theme.
  • Keyboard friendly: every tile, button and drag list works from the keyboard.

Getting started

  1. Install Carnet from the Microsoft Store and open it.
  2. Choose PDF, Word document or Image, then the tool you need.
  3. Drop your file in (or click to choose it), adjust options if any, and press the green button.
  4. Press Download and pick where to save. Your original file is never changed.

Questions

Are my files uploaded anywhere?

No. Carnet does all the work on your PC and has no online features. See the privacy policy.

Does Carnet change my original file?

Never. It always creates a new file, which you save where you like.

Why does "Reorder pages" find only one page in my Word document?

Word works out pages while it displays a document, so Carnet relies on page breaks and the page markers Word saves. Open the document in Microsoft Word, save it once, and try again.

How good is PDF to Word?

Text, fonts, sizes and pictures come across as editable content, one Word page per PDF page. Complex layouts such as tables, columns and charts may need some tidying. Scanned pages are kept as pictures.

Can it open password-protected PDFs?

Not yet. Remove the password in your PDF reader first, then use Carnet.
