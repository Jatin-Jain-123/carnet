Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

Carnet™ is a document toolkit for Windows made by Jatin Jain. This policy covers the Carnet app and this website.

The short version

Carnet collects nothing. Your files are processed on your own PC and never leave it.

Your files

  • Where they're processed: only on your computer. Carnet has no servers and makes no internet connections.
  • Your originals: are only read, never changed or deleted.
  • Results: are kept in memory until you press Download, then saved only where you choose.
  • Temporary files: when converting Word to PDF with Microsoft Word, Carnet writes a temporary copy to your Windows temp folder for Word to open, and deletes it straight after.
  • No history: Carnet doesn't keep a list of the files you've opened.

Other apps on your PC

If Microsoft Word is installed, Carnet asks it to create PDFs from Word documents, on your PC. Word's own privacy settings apply to Word.

What Carnet does not do

  • No accounts, sign-ins or personal information.
  • No analytics, tracking, advertising, cookies or crash reporting.
  • No data is sold or shared, because none is collected.

Microsoft Store

Carnet is installed and updated through the Microsoft Store. Microsoft may collect data about installs, purchases and app health under the Microsoft Privacy Statement. Carnet receives no personal information from this.

This website

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages and uses no cookies or analytics. GitHub may log basic visit information (such as IP addresses) for security; see the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Children

Carnet collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this ever changes (for example, if an optional feature needs the internet), this page will be updated before the change ships, and the feature will be off unless you turn it on.
